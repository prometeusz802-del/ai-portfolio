01 · Generative film

Hellish Gate

2nd Place — Digital Ars 2025 · Open Category 3:44 short film20+ hours1,173 generated scene candidates

The project began with a song I created in Suno. While listening to the lyrics, I imagined a dark western story and started translating that vision into prompts, scenes and a complete visual narrative.

Music & ideaOriginal Suno track became the starting point for the film's story and tone.
Story & storyboardChatGPT was used to develop the concept, storyboard and individual scene prompts.
Generation & iterationVEED's ChatGPT integration supported the generation workflow. I created 1,173 scene candidates.
Edit & directionI selected and assembled the final shots — and scrapped one full version when the narrative did not match the result I wanted.
Result: I submitted the finished film to Digital Ars 2025 and received 2nd place in the Open category. The awards were presented at AI Creative Fest 2025 in Warsaw, where I attended Kamil Soldacki's talk — my first introduction to ElevenLabs.
